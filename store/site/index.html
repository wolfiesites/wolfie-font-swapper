    Questions

    Good to know

    Does it collect any data? +
    No. There's no analytics, no accounts and no server owned by us. Your selections, presets, per-domain rules and language are stored locally in your browser. See the privacy policy.
    Why does it run on all sites? +
    So your saved per-domain font rules can auto-apply when you revisit a site, and so the popup opens on any page. It only reads the page's hostname to match your rules and injects CSS — it never reads or sends page content.
    Can I keep a custom font setup per website? +
    Yes — it's optional. Pin a custom preset to specific domains and it's saved persistently, auto-applying every time you revisit those sites. A green dot on the toolbar icon shows when a domain rule is active; you can remove it anytime from the popup.
    Where do the fonts come from? +
    Google Fonts are fetched from Google's public endpoints only when you preview them. System fonts are read by name (with your one-time permission) and stay on your device.
    How does Pro payment work? +
    Pro is handled by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe. Your email and payment details are processed on their servers — the developer never sees your card data. The free tier never contacts ExtensionPay.
    Which browsers are supported? +
    Chrome and Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera) that support Manifest V3 extensions.
